######################
 Virtues Bibliography
######################

This bibliography supports the virtues and vices section. It is offered
as background reading for the section as a whole.

***************
 Primary texts
***************

-  Aristotle. *Nicomachean Ethics*. Especially Books II, III, VI, VII,
   and X.

-  Thomas Aquinas. *Summa Theologiae*. Especially the Second Part of the
   Second Part on charity, temperance, humility, pride, gluttony, lust,
   anger, continence, and related virtues and vices.

*****************
 Secondary works
*****************

-  Annas, Julia. *Intelligent Virtue*. Oxford University Press, 2011.

-  Foot, Philippa. *Natural Goodness*. Oxford University Press, 2001.

-  Hursthouse, Rosalind. *On Virtue Ethics*. Oxford University Press,
   1999.

-  MacIntyre, Alasdair. *After Virtue*. University of Notre Dame Press,
   1981.

-  Peterson, Christopher, and Martin E. P. Seligman. *Character
   Strengths and Virtues: A Handbook and Classification*. Oxford
   University Press, 2004.

-  Roberts, Robert C. *Emotions: An Essay in Aid of Moral Psychology*.
   Cambridge University Press, 2003.

-  Snow, Nancy E. *Virtue as Social Intelligence: An Empirically
   Grounded Theory*. Routledge, 2010.

   Philosophy*.
-  Hursthouse, Rosalind, and Glen Pettigrove. "Virtue Ethics." *The
